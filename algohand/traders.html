What you need

A VPS, your terminals, ten minutes

A Windows VPS with MT5

Any provider. Your terminals stay exactly as they are, logged in to your accounts, with your EAs and licences.

One installer

Download, run, paste the code from your AlgoHand account. The agent finds every terminal on the machine by itself.

Open the chat

On your phone or laptop. Your robots appear with their real settings — nothing to re-create, nothing to configure.

Keep control

Every change is shown as a dry run first. You approve, the agent applies it, the terminal confirms it. You can always check the audit trail.

What you can ask

Plain questions, plain answers

"How did last week go?"

Closed P&L per robot, win rate, best and worst trade, drawdown — with charts in the chat.

"Pause everything on EURUSD"

Soft stop: the EAs keep managing their open trades and open no new ones. Resume with one message.

"Restart Night Hunter with MaxSpread 15"

The assistant finds the exact input name and its allowed range, shows you the change, and applies it after your yes.

"Is my VPS OK?"

Terminal connected, AutoTrading on, last tick time, memory and disk. If something broke overnight, you hear what the agent did about it.

"Show my balance curve since January"

The whole deal history of the account, drawn as a curve, with the maximum drawdown marked.

"Which hours make money?"

Profit and win rate by hour of day and weekday — the kind of question that used to mean exporting to Excel.

Honest limits

What it does not do

  • It does not trade. It operates the robots you chose; it never opens or closes positions itself.
  • It does not change settings on a grid EA with open trades until that EA's recovery has been verified — you get a clear refusal, not a gamble.
  • It does not give financial advice. Performance figures are information; decisions are yours.
  • It does not need your passwords, and it does not store them.
