How it will work

You describe it once. Every trader gets it right.

Upload the .ex5

Our discovery reads the input names, types and defaults straight from the compiled file on a demo terminal. No source code, ever.

Describe

Write the description traders see, name the presets, mark which inputs may be changed by chat and within which ranges, and which must stay locked.

We verify

We test restart behaviour with open trades on a demo account and review ranges and enum values. You see the results.

Live in the catalog

Your EA appears for traders and partner platforms with your name on it. New build: upload, re-verify, listing history stays.

Why bother

Fewer support tickets. More people using it correctly.

  • Users change only what you allow, within ranges you set — no more "I set the lot to 10 and it blew up"
  • Your presets replace the guesswork of ninety inputs
  • The badge states what you already know: it survives a restart with open trades
  • A new channel: brokers and platforms integrating AlgoHand see your EA in their catalog
Not part of the deal
  • We do not resell, re-license or host your EA — licensing and payment stay with you
  • We do not ask for source code and we do not reverse-engineer compiled files
  • We do not publish performance claims; traders see their own results, not marketing
